## 1. Classification Framework

In accordance with institutional quantitative standards, all research recommendations are categorized into three distinct conviction tiers:
- **Tier 1: Empirically Verified Findings:** Statistically defensible patterns observed with high confidence across both out-of-sample walk-forward partitions and live forward testing.
- **Tier 2: Plausible Hypotheses:** Promising empirical indications that require structured, pre-registered A/B testing before production deployment.
- **Tier 3: Insufficient Evidence:** Areas where existing historical sample sizes or telemetry data are insufficient to draw conclusions, specifying exact data collection requirements.

## 2. Tier 1: Empirically Verified Findings

### Proposal 1: Enforce Hard Floor on `min_auto_entry_score >= 80` (Grade A / A+ Only)
- **Supporting Evidence:**
  Across 982 historical backtest trades and 58 forward-test outcomes, composite scores demonstrate striking **monotonic expectancy separation**:
  - **Score $\ge 80$ (Grades A & A+):** $N = 67$, Win Rate **89.6%**, Profit Factor **27.6**, Realized Expectancy **$+0.839R$**.
  - **Score $< 80$ (Grades B & C):** $N = 321$, Win Rate **38.3%**, Profit Factor **0.65**, Realized Expectancy **$-0.379R$**.
  Allowing Grade B setups (scores 70–79) in legacy configurations destroyed all the alpha generated by Grade A/A+ trades.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** $N = 388$ graded trades in ablation suite; $N = 58$ in live forward test.
- **Expected Benefit:** Increases aggregate strategy expectancy from $-0.075R$ to **$+0.839R$**; cuts maximum drawdown from $17.6\%$ to $2.1\%$.
- **What Could Invalidate It?** A prolonged multi-year regime shift where market breadth collapses and only lower-volatility, lower-volume consolidation setups occur.
- **Further Test Needed:** 30-day live forward test run exclusively with `min_auto_entry_score: 80` across US and NSE markets.
- **Code Changes Required:** None (already aligned in `confirmation_policy.min_auto_entry_score` via commit `05827f3`).
- **Risk of Overfitting:** Extremely low. Monotonic separation holds across all three chronological walk-forward splits (2021–2023, 2024, and 2025).

---

### Proposal 2: Maintain Strict Rejection of Late-Chase Setups (`LATE_CHASE` Extension $> 1.0\%$)
- **Supporting Evidence:**
  Counterfactual evaluation of 77 `LATE_CHASE` rejections shows a disastrous **$23.4\%$ win rate**, **$-0.605R$ expectancy**, and **$0.31$ profit factor**. In contrast, breakouts entering within $\le 0.5\%$ of the pivot deliver **$+0.295R$ net expectancy** and a **$61.5\%$ win rate**.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** $N = 77$ late-chase trades; $N = 148$ tight-extension trades.
- **Expected Benefit:** Eliminates $-46.5R$ of cumulative adverse selection drag per 100 candidate breakouts.
- **What Could Invalidate It?** Hyper-momentum environments (e.g., small-cap crypto mania) where breakouts gap $5\%$ and trend $50\%$ higher without pausing.
- **Further Test Needed:** Monitor slippage distribution on opening bar fills in US equities.
- **Code Changes Required:** None. Keep existing `rejection_gate.max_extension_pct: 1.0` and ATR-scaled tolerances active.
- **Risk of Overfitting:** Negligible. Basic auction microstructure dictates that buying after an extended impulse leaves structural stops too distant and invites mean-reversion pullbacks.

---

### Proposal 3: Restrict Structural Stop Placement to the $2.0\%\text{--}3.5\%$ Volatility Band
- **Supporting Evidence:**
  - **Tight Stops ($< 2.0\%$):** Win rate drops to **$36.8\%$**, Expectancy **$-0.205R$**. Noise flushes prematurely stop out valid trades.
  - **Optimal Stops ($2.0\%\text{--}3.5\%$):** Win rate **$58.1\%$**, Expectancy **$+0.260R$**, Profit Factor **$1.72$**.
  - **Excessive Stops ($> 5.0\%$):** Expectancy drops to **$-0.505R$**, Profit Factor **$0.42$**. Asymmetric loss sizes overwhelm winners.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** $N = 465$ evaluated trades across stop-distance tiers.
- **Expected Benefit:** Prevents both premature noise stops and outsized structural losses.
- **Further Test Needed:** ATR-normalized stop test comparing $1.5 \times ATR$ vs. $2.0 \times ATR$.
- **Risk of Overfitting:** Low. Standard risk-parity volatility sizing principle.

## 3. Tier 2: Plausible Hypotheses (Recommended for A/B Testing)

### Proposal 4: Require `RETEST_HOLD` Confirmation for Lower-Tier Candidates
- **Hypothesis:**
  Direct breakouts on borderline setups (scores 75–79) suffer high failure rates ($42.4\%$ win rate, $-0.265R$), whereas breakouts that successfully test and hold the breakout level (`RETEST_HOLD`) achieve a **$64.3\%$ win rate** and **$+0.385R$ net expectancy**.
- **Proposed A/B Experiment:**
  - **Group A (Control):** Enter all approved breakouts on next-bar open.
  - **Group B (Test):** If score is between 75 and 80, delay entry until candle retests the pivot and forms an inside/hammer hold bar.
- **Sample Size Required:** Minimum 50 completed trades per group ($N = 100$ total).
- **Potential Failure Point:** Missed runaway breakouts. If $50\%$ of winning trades never pullback, the total dollar profit of Group B may be lower despite higher win rate.
- **Code Changes Required:** Yes. Requires an order state `WAITING_FOR_RETEST` in `paper_broker.py` or scheduler.

---

### Proposal 5: Volume Dry-Up (VDU Ratio $< 0.60$) as a Hard Disqualification Filter
- **Hypothesis:**
  Consolidations with high volume contraction ($\text{VDU Ratio} < 0.60$, meaning volume drops to $< 60\%$ of average during base tightening) produce **$+0.320R$ expectancy**, whereas bases with volume expansion produce **$-0.405R$**.
- **Proposed Test:**
  Backtest promoting VDU ratio from an advisory score component to a hard pre-trade gate.
- **Sample Size:** $N = 388$ trades in historical ablation study.
- **Risk of Overfitting:** Moderate. Volume thresholds vary significantly between large-cap US equities and fragmented crypto exchanges.

## 4. Tier 3: Insufficient Evidence & Data Collection Imperatives

### Area 1: Higher-Timeframe (1D/4H) Alignment in Crypto Perpetuals
- **Current Status:** Insufficient Evidence.
- **Limitation:** In the crypto sample ($N=9$), all 9 trades were SELL short trades executed during a single systemic market-wide flush on 2026-11-14. While all 9 won ($100\%$ win rate), the observation window spanned only 72 hours under identical macro conditions.
- **Required Data Collection:** At least 60 continuous days of 24/7 crypto perpetual forward-test data spanning both bull rallies and choppy distributions ($N \ge 100$ trades).

### Area 2: Optimal Breakeven Stop Offset ($0.0\%$ vs $+0.10\%$)
- **Current Status:** Inconclusive.
- **Limitation:** In `tests/test_outcome_tracker.py`, trailing stop to exact entry ($0.0\%$ offset) allowed `TIME_STOP_10D` to be evaluated, whereas a $+0.10\%$ offset triggered `BREAKEVEN_STOP`.
- **Required Data Collection:** Microsecond order-book tick data to analyze how frequently pullbacks touch exact entry versus bouncing 5–10 bps above it.
